About

Wikicollabs is a nonprofit organization based in Jakarta, Indonesia. It is legally registered as Kolaborasi Pengembangan Wiki, a perkumpulan (an association under Indonesian law), established on 12 August 2025.

We develop and maintain open-source software for Wikidata and Linked Open Data.

We contact people only from addresses ending in @wikicollabs.org. Registration details are available on request from support@wikicollabs.org.
